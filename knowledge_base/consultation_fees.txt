Consultation charges follow a tiered specialty pricing model calibrated according to clinical complexity and doctor seniority. General Medicine visits typically start at five hundred Indian Rupees, whereas specialized consultations in Pediatrics and Dermatology range from eight hundred to fifteen hundred Rupees. Super-specialty consultations in Cardiology and Orthopedics range between twelve hundred and twenty-five hundred Rupees depending on required diagnostic screenings. All published fees cover a comprehensive initial clinical assessment, medical chart entry, and digital prescription issuance.
