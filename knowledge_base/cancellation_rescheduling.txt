Patients may cancel or reschedule any confirmed appointment without penalty up to two hours before the designated consultation time. Cancellations made inside the two-hour window will incur a late cancellation charge equal to twenty percent of the consultation fee. Rescheduling is permissible up to twice per booking without forfeiting initial payments, subject to slot availability. In the event of an unavoidable physician emergency, patients receive an instant full refund and priority rebooking assistance.
