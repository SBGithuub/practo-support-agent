Standard diagnostic screenings including complete blood counts, urinalysis, and metabolic panels are processed within four to six hours of sample collection. Specialized biochemical assays, autoimmune panels, and microbiological cultures require twenty-four to forty-eight hours for comprehensive incubation and pathology verification. High-resolution imaging studies such as MRI and CT scans are delivered with signed radiologist interpretations within twelve hours. Patients receive automated real-time notifications with secure cloud download links as soon as laboratory results are certified.
