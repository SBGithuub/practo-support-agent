Patients experiencing sudden crushing chest discomfort, severe dyspnea, acute neurological weakness, profound hemorrhaging, or major trauma should immediately bypass routine booking channels and proceed to the nearest emergency trauma department. The support agent and online chat interfaces are strictly non-emergent informational systems and cannot dispatch rapid medical rescue teams. In severe life-threatening emergencies, patients or relatives must dial the national emergency ambulance hotline at 108 or 112 without delay. Emergency room facilities operate continuously twenty-four hours a day with dedicated critical-care triage physicians.
