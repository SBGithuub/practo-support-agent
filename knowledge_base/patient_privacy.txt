All electronic health records, diagnostic imaging, and consultation transcripts are stored with military-grade AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit. Patient health data is shared strictly with attending medical practitioners directly involved in the patient episode of care. Practo adheres strictly to healthcare data protection norms and will never monetize, lease, or distribute private medical histories to commercial marketing entities. Patients maintain full autonomous rights to review access logs, restrict record sharing, or request permanent chart pseudonymization at any time.
