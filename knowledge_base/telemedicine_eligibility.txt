Telemedicine video appointments are available for non-urgent routine consultations, test report reviews, and minor acute follow-up discussions. First-time consultations requiring direct tactile physical examination, palpation, or auscultation are ineligible for virtual appointments and must be held in clinic. Patients must possess a stable broadband internet connection and join from a well-lit, private room to maintain consultation quality. The examining doctor reserves the unilateral clinical right to convert any video call into an in-person visit if clinical red flags emerge.
