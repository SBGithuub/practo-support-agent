Cashless insurance claims are supported across all network-affiliated hospitals for inpatient admissions and scheduled outpatient day-care procedures. Patients must submit a valid health insurance card and government photographic identification at the front desk at least one hour before admission. For standalone outpatient consultations, patients can download stamped digital expense receipts and diagnostic invoices directly from their Practo dashboard for reimbursement filing. Reimbursement claims are processed by accredited third-party administrators within seven to fourteen business days.
