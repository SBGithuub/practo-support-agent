Home healthcare and bedside physician visits are available for bedridden elderly individuals, post-operative orthopedic recovery patients, and immunocompromised individuals unable to commute. Eligible patients must reside within a designated fifteen-kilometer perimeter of a participating network medical center. Certified home health nurses accompanied by general practitioners carry portable diagnostic kits for point-of-care vital checks, basic phlebotomy, and wound dressing. Home visit requests must be scheduled at least four hours in advance through the dedicated homecare concierge desk.
